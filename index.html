// 01 · Game Overview

Become Bond

007 First Light is James Bond's origin story — developed by IO Interactive (Hitman series). You play as 26-year-old James Bond, voiced and performed by Patrick Gibson, before he earns his 00 status. Spanning 12 missions across the globe, the game blends social stealth, gadget-based infiltration, and explosive action. Expect ~20 hours for the main story, up to 40 with extras and TacSim mode.

// 02 · Mission Walkthroughs

Every Mission, Every Secret

Pre-Launch Notice: The game has 12 missions total. Detailed walkthroughs for Missions 1–2 are confirmed from preview coverage. Missions 3–7 are based on preview content and will be expanded post-launch. Missions 8–12 have not been publicly detailed yet — full guides coming after May 27, 2026.
M-01

Slovakia Chess Tournament

Grand Carpathian Hotel, Slovakia Medium

Bond's first field assignment. Infiltrate the Grand Carpathian Hotel during a high-stakes chess tournament to track rogue agent 009. This mission teaches the core stealth and spycraft mechanics.

Walkthrough:

  • Observe first — Stay in public areas and watch guard patrols before acting.
  • Eavesdrop — Conversations reveal staff schedules and keycard locations. This intel is critical.
  • Q-Watch Laser — Use it on locked doors. It's silent and leaves no evidence. Prefer it over hunting for keycards.
  • Staff shortcut — The kitchen service corridor (learned via eavesdropping) bypasses two checkpoints.
  • Don't dart in public — Civilians witness tranquilizer shots and raise alarms, forcing Intinct bluffs.
  • Final confrontation — The mission ends in unavoidable combat. The game teaches that stealth isn't always an option.

Key Gadgets: Q-Watch (laser cutter), Sleeping Dart

M-02

Uninvited

Kensington, London Medium

Social infiltration at a corporate gala. Bond arrives with no valid cover identity and must build credentials from scratch inside the venue. The Smoke Pod is unlocked here.

Walkthrough:

  • Best path: Photographer impersonation — Find the real photographer's dropped name badge in the east cloakroom. Use it to bluff past lower-floor checkpoints.
  • Security chief — Requires more intel or an Instinct spend to bypass.
  • Stairwell double takedown — Use the Smoke Pod for concealment, then melee both guards simultaneously.
  • Garden extraction is a movement puzzle — Follow Spycraft observation markers. Don't draw a weapon. You can exit in under 60 seconds with zero alerts.
  • Fighting the garden triggers reinforcements from the gala, turning a 60-second exit into a 15-minute firefight.

Tip: Pickpocketing costs 0 Instinct. Bluffing costs 1. Always pickpocket first.

M-03

Against All Odds PREVIEW

Iceland Hard

Helicopter crash survival in Iceland's frozen landscape. One of the game's most visually striking missions. Stealth is heavily favored — gunshots carry far across open terrain.

  • Use Q-Watch thermal scan to detect enemies through snowdrifts and walls.
  • Your visibility is reduced in snow — enemies also have limited sight range. Use this symmetry.
  • XP and optional loot hidden in wreckage — explore before proceeding.

Tip: Don't engage in prolonged firefights. The open terrain leaves you exposed. Reposition constantly.

M-04

A New Home PREVIEW

Malta Easy

Training simulation mission set in Malta. A breather level that introduces advanced gadget mechanics and the TacSim framework.

  • Complete all optional training objectives to unlock bonus starting gear for subsequent missions.
  • This is the best place to practice multi-target smoke + melee takedowns.

Tip: Replay this mission to experiment with gadget combos risk-free.

M-05

Monaco Yacht PREVIEW

Monaco Hard

The most complex mission in the game. A luxury yacht in Monaco's harbor — multiple decks, tight corridors, and heavy guard presence. Classic Bond setting: glamour, casinos, and danger.

  • Underwater hull access — A submerged entry point bypasses topside security entirely.
  • Laundry chutes — Use them to move between decks undetected.
  • Tuxedo reduces suspicion in the onboard casino area.
  • Multiple disguise opportunities and NPC interaction trees.
  • Vehicle chase sequence on the coastal highway after infiltration.

Tip: Bring the Dart Phone. It's the cleanest non-lethal option in crowded social spaces.

M-06

Neon Labyrinth PREVIEW

Tokyo Hard

Tokyo's dense urban environment turns this mission into a vertical stealth puzzle. Multiple floors, neon-lit crowds, and heavy electronic surveillance.

  • Hack security cameras with the Q-Watch before entering restricted zones — looping feeds reduces patrol frequency.
  • The Explosive Pen is essential here — several locked rooms require blowing adjacent wall panels.
  • Use crowds as cover in the street-level sections; avoid drawing your pistol near civilians.

Tip: The Q-Watch hacking tool gets its most demanding use in this mission. Recharge between segments.

M-07

Desert Reckoning PREVIEW

Dubai Hard

The climactic confrontation against Bawma (Lenny Kravitz), the game's primary antagonist. Dubai's skyline provides the backdrop for 007 First Light's most demanding sequence.

  • All gadgets and combat skills converge here — your loadout choices across previous missions matter.
  • Multiple approach paths to the final encounter. Stealth, loud, or a mix — all are viable with proper preparation.
  • Replay earlier missions to unlock Expert modifiers — the rewards directly benefit the finale.

// 04 · Q Branch Gadgets

Tools of the Trade

Note: Unlock conditions (e.g. "Mid-game unlock", "After Mission 4") are approximate based on preview coverage. Exact unlock requirements will be confirmed post-launch. Additional gadgets may also be present in the full game that weren't shown in pre-release materials.

Q-Lens

Available from start

Bond's reconnaissance tool. Highlights hackable devices, enemies through walls, interactive objects, and patrol routes. Hold L1 (or equivalent) to scan every room before entry.

Scan every single room before entering. It reveals enemy counts, camera positions, and hidden loot you'd otherwise miss.

Q-Watch (OMEGA)

Mission 1 · Always Active

Bond's primary multi-tool. Functions include: laser cutter (silent lock breaking, cutting cables), camera loop (disables camera feeds for 45 seconds without alert), chandelier/environmental trigger, and rope cutting. Also houses the Sleeping Dart launcher.

Use the laser on locked doors before searching for keycards. It's silent, permanent, and never triggers suspicion.

Sleeping Dart (Q-Watch)

Mission 1

Silent single-target non-lethal takedown integrated into the Q-Watch. Targets slump silently with no reaction noise. 10-12 second reload cycle — treat it as a one-shot-per-engagement tool, not a chain option.

Don't use in public atriums — civilian witnesses can report it. Save for interior guards only.

Smoke Pod

Mission 2 (London Gala)

Creates a 4-5 second visual obstruction. Use it to set up multi-target melee takedowns, reposition under pressure, or create confusion in tight spaces. Essential for the stairwell encounter in Mission 2.

Pair with melee for simultaneous double takedowns. Smoke + melee is faster and quieter than darts for grouped guards.

Dart Phone

Available from start

Smartphone modified to fire tranquilizer darts at range. Non-lethal takedown with no body reaction noise. Ideal for Monaco's crowded social spaces. The cleanest ranged non-lethal option.

Tag targets with Q-Lens first, tranq from distance, move on. Fastest stealth clear method in the game.

Laser Strap

Mid-game unlock

A versatile gadget that dazes enemies, opens new paths, or creates environmental distractions. Can knock down chandeliers and other hanging objects onto enemies for accident kills.

Accident kills (chandelier drops, electrified puddles) count as environmental — bodies can be found without voiding Silent Assassin rating.

Flash Mine

Mid-game unlock

Deployable mine that produces a burst of light on activation, momentarily stunning all enemies in radius. Useful for crowd control during combat or as a distraction to slip past patrols.

Place around corners or at chokepoints. The stun window is generous — enough for a full room clear.

EMP Watch

Later mission unlock

Loops camera feeds for 45 seconds, disabling all electronic surveillance in a radius. **Do not shoot cameras** — the EMP Watch approach is silent and leaves no evidence.

Three cameras at once? One EMP charge clears them all. Essential for the Tokyo mission's vertical stealth sections.

Hacking Device

After Mission 4

Portable terminal hacker. Opens digital-locked doors and accesses restricted terminals. Paired with the Q-Phone for extended functionality.

Some locked rooms can only be opened by hacking. Always check if a door can be hacked before looking for keys.

Spy Drone

After Mission 1

Reconnaissance drone. Marks guards, cameras, patrol routes, and alternate entry points from a safe distance. Saves time and Instinct by revealing the full floor plan before Bond moves in.

Deploy before entering any major new area. The intel it provides makes Silent Assassin runs significantly easier.

Sonic Emitter Coin

Mid-game unlock

Remote distraction device disguised as a coin. Creates a noise at the target location, drawing guards away from their posts. Works through walls and around corners.

The most reliable way to split up paired guards. Toss one down a corridor, take the straggler, then circle back.

Tranquilizer Pen

Later mission unlock

Pen-shaped injection device for silent takedowns on armored targets. Bypasses body armor that sleeping darts cannot penetrate.

Armored guards are immune to regular darts. The Tranq Pen is your only non-lethal option against them — conserve it.

// 05 · Beginner's Guide

Play Like a 00 Agent

Pre-Launch Note: Tips and mechanics in this section are compiled from preview coverage and hands-on reports (Eurogamer, Kotaku, GameRant, etc.). While we believe these are accurate, some specifics may change in the final release build. We will verify and update everything after May 27, 2026.

Master the four pillars — Spycraft, Instinct, Gadgets, and Combat — with these essential tips for new players. Spycraft is always free: eavesdropping, pickpocketing, and environmental observation cost nothing.

8-Step Entry Protocol for Every New Area

01

Observe Before You Act

30 seconds of watching guard patrols saves 5 minutes of backtracking. Use the Q-Lens to scan the room through walls. Know enemy counts, camera positions, and patrol routes before moving.

02

Eavesdrop on Every Conversation

Ambient NPC conversations contain guard schedules, keycard locations, passcodes, and even full alternate route information. Grey optional dialogue markers almost always unlock a better path.

03

Use the Q-Watch Laser on Doors First

Before hunting for keycards, try the watch laser. It's silent, permanent, and never registers as suspicious activity. Only search for keycards when the laser can't open something.

04

Pickpocket Before You Bluff

Pickpocketing keycards from distracted guards costs 0 Instinct. Bluffing costs 1 Instinct unit. Always pickpocket first. A guard in conversation is a free pickpocket window.

05

Build Instinct Through Optional Objectives

Instinct refills come from completing optional mission objectives, not just main markers. Go out of your way to complete them — you'll need Instinct for critical bluffs and Focus moments later.

06

Shoot Legs, Not Center Mass

Shooting guards in the legs incapacitates them without triggering "License to Kill" (lethal escalation mode). Once License to Kill is active, it persists for the entire mission segment.

07

Bluff Before You Dart

If a guard is suspicious but not fully alerted, a successful Instinct bluff resets them to calm for free. Only dart if the bluff fails or you don't have Instinct to spare.

08

Replay Mission 1 After Mission 2

New routes and gadgets unlocked in London open up additional paths in the Slovakia mission. Replaying with your expanded arsenal yields new intel and collectibles.

The Situational Decision Tree

No guards aware → Spycraft first (observe, eavesdrop, find shortcuts) Single guard blocking path → Q-Watch (laser for doors, sleeping dart for guards) Guard suspicious but not alerted → Bluff via Instinct Guard alerted, others unaware → Smoke + melee takedown Multiple guards alerted → Reposition, use environmental hazards Full alert, enemies armed → Commit to combat fully

5 Common Mistakes New Players Make

!

Drawing the Silenced Pistol Too Early

Any drawn weapon raises nearby NPC awareness to Yellow Alert — even if you don't fire. Use the Q-Watch Sleeping Dart instead. It's quieter and doesn't escalate the area.

!

Skipping Eavesdrop Conversations

Grey optional markers are not filler. They contain critical path information: alternative routes, guard shift changes, and key locations. Players who skip them often hit dead ends.

!

Wasting Instinct on Bluffs

A guard in conversation is a free pickpocket window. Always exhaust free options (Spycraft) before spending resources (Instinct). Pickpocketing costs zero; bluffing costs one.

!

Triggering License to Kill Unnecessarily

Once active, License to Kill doesn't deactivate mid-mission. It escalates all future encounters to lethal force. Only engage it when the game forces you into combat.

!

Fighting Through the Mission 2 Garden Extraction

The garden extraction is a movement puzzle, not a combat encounter. Follow Spycraft observation markers and don't draw your weapon. You can exit in 60 seconds with zero alerts. Fighting takes 15+ minutes.

Stealth & Detection Mechanics

AI Vision

110° primary cone, 160° peripheral. Stay low and use cover-to-cover movement.

Sound Radius

Sprinting = 15m noise radius. Crouch-walk within 8m of enemies to stay silent.

Body Hiding

Unconscious bodies must go in lockers, dumpsters, or deep shadows (0% light). Bodies found in the open void Silent Assassin.

Camera Protocol

Don't shoot cameras. Use EMP Watch to loop feeds for 45 seconds. Shooting triggers alerts.

Accident Kills

Chandelier drops, electrified puddles = environmental kills. Bodies can be found without voiding Silent Assassin.

Wardrobe Matters

Dark turtleneck = 40% less fabric rustling. White tuxedo in shadows = 15% faster detection.
